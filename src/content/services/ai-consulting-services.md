## The honest position on this term

"AI consulting" is one of the most contested phrases in this market. The first page of results
is EY, IBM, Slalom, BCG and Accenture, and they are there because they run programmes with
budgets and governance attached.

We are not competing for that work and we would lose if we tried. What we do is the narrow
slice they usually scope out: the operational path from a stranger arriving on your site to a
rep having a useful conversation.

If you arrived here looking for enterprise transformation, the large firms are the correct
answer and this page has saved you a call.

## What we assess

Four counts, reconciled: requests that arrived, records created, records with a live owner,
records actually worked. The gaps between them are where the money is, and they are almost
never where the org chart says they are.

That reconciliation method is set out in the
[inbound lead audit](/guides/inbound-lead-audit/).

## The ranking rule

Opportunities get ranked by the cost of the current failure, not by how interesting the fix is.
A routing rule with no fallback branch is dull and often the single most expensive defect in
the funnel. A conversational agent is interesting and frequently worth less.

Consultants who rank by novelty produce roadmaps that look impressive and start with the third
most valuable item.

## Sequencing is part of the advice

Launching three modules in one month means you cannot attribute the change to any of them. You
will have moved a number and learned nothing about which lever did it.

We sequence deliberately, with an exit test per step, so the second decision is better informed
than the first. Where lead ops ends and RevOps begins, which determines who owns each artefact,
is covered in [lead ops versus RevOps](/guides/lead-ops-vs-revops/).

## Where this advice stops being worth buying

If you already know what to build and have engineers idle, buy the build. If your inbound
volume is small enough that one person reads every request, the leak is visible without
analysis.

And if nobody will own the rules after we leave, no assessment survives contact with the next
quarter. We will raise that on the call rather than in an invoice.
