## Where does OperStack do more than Peec AI for a small business?

For a business owner who wants to be named by ChatGPT, OperStack delivers the finished work rather than the data behind it: a list of who ChatGPT picked instead, a score for ten of your pages, and the paragraph to paste, for $35 a month against $95 for Peec's cheapest plan. OperStack is our product. The Peec AI facts below come from Peec's own pages and documentation, read on 26 September 2026 and linked in the table above.

**1. Your pages get read and scored.** The OperStack [free check](/ai-visibility/) takes your address, picks up to ten pages from your sitemap, and gives them a score out of 100 for whether an AI system can open, identify and quote them. Watch repeats that on ten pages every week. How many of your pages Peec reads is not stated: its docs describe a site audit group inside Actions and a robots.txt check for AI bots, without a page count.

**2. You get the paragraph, not the brief.** Peec's Actions list ranks what to work on and gives each item "The brief" for structure and "What to do" steps. Its own FAQ answers yes to the question of whether you still create the content yourself. OperStack writes it: when a page has no short answer near the top, the free check drafts one from the facts on your own pages, and Watch sends a fix list for the whole site with each page address and its text. Every sentence comes from what your own site already says.

**3. There is no prompt list to build.** Peec asks you to write or choose your prompts, 50 of them on Starter, then pick three models. Watch uses ten questions of the kind a buyer of your type of business asks, and keeps them fixed from week to week so the trend means something.

**4. You can see a result before any account exists.** Peec has no free plan, and we found no public free checker on its site or in its sitemap; the trial starts with an account through Google, Microsoft, a work email link or SSO. The OperStack free check needs nothing: it runs three live ChatGPT questions with web search on and shows who was named, split into three groups: real rivals, names that are not rivals (portals, law firms and the like), and names it could not place.

**5. The weekly report arrives by email.** A built-in weekly email is not stated on Peec's pages. Its week-over-week digest, called Weekly Visibility Pulse, is an MCP prompt you run inside Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, or schedule yourself as a task that posts to Slack. Watch simply emails you every 7 days.

**6. The price is about a third of Peec Starter's.** On our [pricing page](/pricing/), Watch is listed at $35 a month, or $299 for a full year. Peec Starter is $95 billed monthly or $960 a year billed annually (our arithmetic from $80 a month), and a fourth AI model adds $30 a month.

**7. Nothing to connect.** Peec's bot analytics need a CDN or log connection and its referral numbers need GA4. Watch needs a site address. Its fixes are almost all words to paste, and anything that truly needs a developer is labeled.

**8. Leaving is simple.** Watch charges nothing for the first 7 days and stops whenever you cancel. On a Peec 12-month plan, the terms ask for 30 days' notice before the year ends, and there is no automatic refund for ending a fixed term early.

## What is Peec AI for, and what is OperStack for?

Peec AI is an AI search analytics platform for marketing teams and agencies; OperStack is a weekly ChatGPT check with the fixes written out, built for owners of small and mid-size businesses.

### Peec AI, in plain words

Peec AI is a German company, launched in February 2025, whose terms fall under German law and Berlin courts. You enter a brand and a domain, add the prompts your customers might type, choose three AI models, and Peec runs every prompt once every 24 hours on each model. The dashboard reports Visibility, Position and Sentiment against the competitors you track, lists the sources AI answers cite, and ranks next steps in Actions. Peec's docs say it collects answers through browser automation of the logged-out web interface rather than through model APIs. Its terms exclude consumers: it sells to businesses only, with plans aimed at SEO and content managers, marketing teams, global brands and agencies.

### OperStack, in plain words

OperStack starts from the site, not from a prompt list. The free check scores ten pages and asks ChatGPT three buyer questions. Watch turns that into a weekly letter: ten questions to ChatGPT, three rivals measured beside you, ten pages re-scored, and the fix list with the text to paste. It asks ChatGPT only, and it carries no keyword or backlink data.

## Is Peec AI worth it for a single small business?

For one business without a marketer, usually not: Peec supplies data and a brief, and by its own FAQ the content is yours to create. A small business owner needs the finished paragraph and the page it goes on, which is what Watch sends for $35 a month.

Consider what Starter asks of a dental clinic or a two-partner law firm. First, a list of 50 prompts, written or chosen, and a choice of three models. The clinic still has to write the new text, because Peec's FAQ says so plainly. Starter collects 4,500 AI answers a month; the open question for a small business is who turns them into changes on the site.

Watch removes those steps. The ten questions are chosen for your kind of business, the names instead come straight from ChatGPT's answers, the score shows which of ten pages is weak and why, and the letter carries the words to paste. If the owner does nothing but read the email and paste the fix of the week, the site still gets one concrete change every seven days.

A fair way to decide is to start with the free result. Run the [free check](/ai-visibility/) on your address. If ChatGPT already names you in all three answers and your pages score well, you may need neither tool yet. If it names someone else, the check shows who, and Watch keeps that list current every week.
