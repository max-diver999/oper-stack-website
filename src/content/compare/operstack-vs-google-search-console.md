Google Search Console is free, it is Google's own record of your site, and every business with a website should have it set up. OperStack does not replace a single report in it. OperStack is our product, and it answers the three questions Search Console was never designed to answer: is your business in ChatGPT's answer when a buyer asks, which firms appear there in its place, and which words on which page would change that. Since June 2026 Search Console also counts impressions in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, so it is fair to ask whether that is enough on its own. Below: what Google's new report counts, what it leaves out, and how the two tools share one weekly routine.

### Whether ChatGPT recommends you at all

Search Console's view of AI ends at Google's border: AI Overviews and AI Mode in Search, plus the generative features in Discover, which have their own report. None of the help pages we read mention ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or the Gemini app. OperStack Watch goes straight to ChatGPT with ten questions a buyer of your kind of business would type, web search switched on, and the wording held steady from one week to the next. Because the questions do not move, a different answer means something did, on your site or on a rival's. The [free check](/ai-visibility/) asks three of those questions before you pay or register anything.

### The names ChatGPT gives in your place

Search Console reports only on properties you have verified or been granted access to, and the docs we checked describe no competitor data and no feature that names other sites. Watch records every business ChatGPT names in each answer and scores three rivals beside you on the same 100-point scale, so you see not just that you were left out but whose pages were preferred. In the free check the names are split three ways, real competitors, not-competitors such as law firms or portals, and names it could not identify, which keeps a directory from being read as a rival.

### A fix you can paste

When Google spots a problem, Search Console names the issue, lists the affected URLs, links to its documentation and lets you mark the repair with "Validate fix". Its Recommendations feature, announced as an experiment in August 2024, suggests actions such as adding structured data or a sitemap. It never hands you words for the page. OperStack does. Where a page lacks a short, direct answer near the top, the free check writes an opening paragraph from facts already on your own site and puts a "Copy the text" button under it. Watch extends that across the site: every item names the page and gives the text, and the handful that need a developer carry a flag.

### Ten pages in one pass, with no account

URL Inspection looks at one address at a time, with a daily cap per property; through the API the cap is 2,000 inspections a day per site. The OperStack free check reads up to ten pages sampled from your sitemap in a single run, scores how easily AI systems can read and quote them, and leaves a permanent link to the result. There is no Google account to connect and no ownership to prove. Search Console, by contrast, asks for an HTML file, a meta tag, an Analytics or Tag Manager code, a Google Sites or Blogger account, or a DNS record. Google calls DNS "more complex", yet it is the only method that verifies a Domain property.

### A letter that comes to you

Search Console writes when something breaks: email alerts for issues Google finds, set per user on the Email preferences page. The help pages we checked describe no regular performance summary by email; the weekly and monthly views sit inside the Performance report, waiting for you to open them. ## What is Search Console for, and what is OperStack for?

Search Console is Google's free record of how your site appears in Google Search; OperStack is AI visibility software that checks how ChatGPT treats your business and tells you what to fix.

### Google Search Console

Google describes it as a service to monitor, maintain and troubleshoot your site's presence in its search results. At its centre is the Performance report: clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position for the queries and pages that showed your site, kept for 16 months. Around that sit Page indexing, URL Inspection with a live test and indexing requests, sitemap submission, a Links report, email alerts and an API. Google's own advice is that a business owner should know what it offers even if they never operate it themselves.

### OperStack

OperStack has two parts. The free check samples up to ten pages, scores them out of 100 across crawler access, llms.txt, entity markup, quotable content, and dates and sources, spells out one fix in full and puts three buyer questions to ChatGPT. OperStack Watch runs a larger version every week; the [pricing page](/pricing/) lists it at $35 a month or $299 a year with seven days free. OperStack holds no search data of any kind: no clicks, impressions, positions, keyword volumes, backlinks or indexing controls. For all of that, Search Console is the source.

## Does Google Search Console show AI Overviews traffic?

It shows impressions, not traffic: the Generative AI performance report, launched on 3 June 2026 and available to all websites worldwide as of 31 August 2026, counts how often links to your site were shown in AI Overviews and AI Mode on Google Search.

| The Generative AI report gives you | It does not give you |
| --- | --- |
| Impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode | Clicks or click-through rate |
| A split by page, country and device | The searches that triggered the appearance |
| Dates by hour, day, week or month | The other sites cited in the same answer |
| A search type filter and an export | Search Labs experiments, which are excluded |
| Discover's AI features, in a separate report | Anything from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or the Gemini app |

Two details decide how you read it. The first is clicks. In the ordinary Performance report, AI Overviews and AI Mode are counted inside the Web search type: an AI Overview takes one position, every link inside it shares that position, and a click on one of those links is a normal click. So clicks from AI answers are already in your totals; the Generative AI report just does not break them out. The second is availability. The report needs a verified property and may not appear for a site with too few AI impressions, so a missing report can mean low exposure rather than a setup fault.

For anyone pulling data by code there is one more gap. The Search Analytics API lists the search types web, image, video, news, googleNews and discover, with no generative AI type, so API access to the new report is not stated. Google's page "AI features and your website", last updated on 10 December 2025, still describes only the older arrangement in which AI features sit inside Web.

Open the report every week, and read it as what it is: Google's count of how often it showed you inside its own AI answers, on its own search engine.

## Does Google Search Console track ChatGPT?

No. Search Console covers Google's surfaces only, and the documentation we checked never mentions ChatGPT, so it cannot tell you whether ChatGPT recommends you, whom it recommends instead or what to change.

A buyer who asks ChatGPT for a shortlist of accountants, clinics or software tools gets names in a chat window, and nothing about that conversation reaches your Search Console account. If ChatGPT then sends the buyer to your site, the visit did not come from Google Search either, so Search Console does not report it. OperStack approaches this from both ends. Watch puts the questions to ChatGPT directly and records who was named, and our separate free [visit counter](/visits/) counts visits that arrive from AI systems.

The gap matters most where buyers pick from a short list: a patient choosing between three clinics, a founder choosing between three tools, a family choosing between three law firms. Appearing in Google's AI Overview for a question helps. Being the name ChatGPT gives when someone asks for a recommendation is a separate measure, and only a tool that asks ChatGPT can see it. If you have been doing that by hand, our page on [asking ChatGPT yourself](/compare/operstack-vs-asking-chatgpt/) covers what a manual check misses.

On a Monday Search Console has fresh numbers waiting but sends nothing unless Google found a problem; the OperStack letter arrives by itself every seven days from the day you subscribed, so it lands on Monday only if that is your cycle day.

An OperStack Monday means opening an email. The two Mondays fit together. Search Console tells you what Google did last week. OperStack tells you what ChatGPT said last week and hands you the next change to make.

**An online shop.** Here Search Console carries most of the weight. ## How do you use Search Console and OperStack together?

Keep Search Console as the ledger of Google and OperStack as the weekly ChatGPT check; there is nothing to switch, only a routine to set up.

1. **Verify a Domain property with a DNS record.** It is the one method Google accepts for that property type.
2. **Submit your sitemap, and keep it findable.** A sitemap submitted in Search Console is known to Google, but that submission reaches Google alone. Our check looks for a sitemap at the usual paths and in robots.txt, and 42% of the sites in our study had none there, or one that would not open.
3. **Run the OperStack free check.** Note the rivals ChatGPT named and the paragraph the check wrote.
4. **Publish the fix, then request indexing** of that page with URL Inspection.
5. **Watch two signals on the same change.** Over the next weeks, that page's impressions in the Generative AI report show Google's side; the named-or-not line in your Watch letter shows ChatGPT's.
6. **Once a month, compare wording.** Set the real queries from the Performance report beside the ten questions in your Watch letter. Where buyers use words in Google that your main page never uses, the page is missing an answer both systems could quote.

If you work in an AI app, both tools can live in one setup: a community server for Search Console data, and OperStack's free MCP server, with [setup notes](/mcp/) for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf and Zed.
