## Where does OperStack do more than the HubSpot AI Search Grader?

OperStack reads your pages, names the businesses ChatGPT recommends in your place, writes the paragraph to paste and repeats all of it every week. HubSpot's pages describe none of those four things for the grader, which is a one-time brand snapshot.

**It reads ten of your pages, not a description of your brand.** The grader takes a company name, a location, an industry and a product, and HubSpot describes no crawl of your site. The [OperStack free check](/ai-visibility/) samples up to 10 pages from your sitemap and scores each one on crawler access, llms.txt, schema, quotable content and dates. The one-off $149 audit goes to 100 pages.

**It shows who beat you, by name.** The free check puts three buyer-style questions to ChatGPT and lists the businesses named in each answer, split into real competitors, bystanders such as portals or law firms, and names it could not place. The grader has a Share of Voice part worth 10 of its 100 points; whether the free report names those competitors is not stated on the page we read.

**It writes the words.** When a checked page opens without a short, plain answer, the free check drafts one for that page using only claims found on your own pages, with a "Copy the text" button. The grader gives a written interpretation and, according to HubSpot's AEO page, "a sample of recommendations".

**It asks today's ChatGPT, with search on.** HubSpot's AEO page says the grader's ChatGPT and Gemini answers come from training data. OperStack asks ChatGPT with web search switched on, the mode that finds what is on the web now.

**It needs no form.** You type a site address and the result appears on screen with a permanent link. The grader's full report needs six fields, phone number included.

**It keeps going every week.** OperStack Watch asks the same ten questions every seven days, measures three rivals beside you, re-scores ten pages and sends one email saying what changed. The grader is a snapshot you have to remember to repeat.

**It costs less and lets you leave.** Watch is $35 a month or $299 a year on the [pricing page](/pricing/), against $50 a month for standalone HubSpot AEO. OperStack's MCP server is free; HubSpot's AI data over MCP needs Marketing Hub Professional, from $800 a month plus $3,000 onboarding. Watch can be cancelled at any time. HubSpot's paid plans end only at the close of the term. And nearly every OperStack fix is a block of text: the handful that need a developer carry a label saying so.

## What are the two tools for?

HubSpot's grader is a brand reputation report built to lead into HubSpot's paid AEO product. OperStack is a page-level check plus weekly ChatGPT tracking for small and mid-size businesses and agencies.

### The HubSpot AI Search Grader

HubSpot launched the grader on 26 February 2025 and describes it as a free, one-time check of what ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini say about a brand. The old address, hubspot.com/aeo-grader, now redirects to hubspot.com/ai-search-grader, which is why some HubSpot pages still say "AEO Grader". It returns a score out of 100 in five parts, with a written interpretation, and HubSpot says the report is ready in under two minutes. The grader page names its audience as marketing leaders, brand managers, SEO professionals and content strategists. The paid follow-on, HubSpot AEO, is labelled Beta and runs tracked prompts daily.

### OperStack

OperStack answers a narrower, more practical question for an owner: when a buyer asks ChatGPT about your kind of business, are you on the list, who is on it instead, and what should change on which page. The free check answers it once, for three questions and up to ten pages. Watch answers it every week for ten questions and keeps the history.

## What does the HubSpot grader ask for before it shows the full report?

The first screen needs only company details. The full report unlocks after a second form, marked "All fields are required", that asks for first name, last name, email, phone number, website URL and number of employees.

We read those fields from the public form definition the grader page loads; its submit button reads "Download Now". We did not submit it, so this page shows no HubSpot result for any site, ours included. The form's privacy text says HubSpot uses the details to contact you about its content, products and services. A phone number is still part of the price of the full report, and the privacy text tells you to expect contact.

The OperStack free check asks for the site address and nothing else. The result appears on screen with a permanent link you can forward to a colleague or a client.

The grader asks one OpenAI model, Perplexity and Gemini. Which OpenAI model depends on the HubSpot page you read. On 26 September 2026 we found three different answers:

A training-data answer reflects what a model absorbed about your brand before its cutoff. OperStack asks ChatGPT with web search switched on, and OpenAI's help page says ChatGPT may search by itself whenever a question needs current information. For a buyer asking for a shortlist today, the searched answer is the one that reflects the web as it stands.

## Is the HubSpot AI Search Grader accurate?

It is a fair reading of what it sets out to measure: how AI models talk about a brand in general. It cannot tell you what is wrong with your website, because HubSpot describes no crawl of your pages, so nothing in the report is tied to a page address.

Three details decide how to read the number.

**The weights lean on sentiment.** Sentiment is worth up to 40 points, Presence Quality 20, Brand Recognition 20, Share of Voice 10 and Market Competition 10. The total therefore says more about how warmly a brand is described than about whether it lands on a buyer's shortlist, which is mostly the job of the 10-point Share of Voice part. HubSpot scores that part as your estimated percentage of category voice plus a rank bonus.

**Rival names are not promised.** The grader page says Share of Voice shows "where your brand ranks relative to competitors". Whether the free report lists those competitors by name is not stated.

**It is one sample.** [SparkToro and Gumshoe's January 2026 research](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) showed that AI tools seldom repeat the same set of brands for an identical prompt, so any single reading carries noise. The same caution applies to our free check, which rests on three ChatGPT answers. That is exactly why Watch exists: the same ten questions, every week, so a trend replaces a guess.

## How does OperStack check AI visibility, page by page?

OperStack starts with your pages and only then asks ChatGPT. The score comes from what the pages contain; the names come from the answers.

| Area of the score | Points | What it looks at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access | 25 | robots.txt rules, agent by agent, for 14 AI crawlers and fetchers |
| Agent map | 15 | Whether llms.txt exists and works |
| Entity | 20 | Organization or business schema, FAQPage, page type, canonical, Open Graph |
| Quotable content | 25 | Length of 300 words or more, a plain answer with a number directly under the H1, three or more H2 headings, a table |
| Dates and sources | 15 | Dates in meta or schema, phrases naming a source, sitemap lastmod |

On top of that, the same pages get an SEO basics score and a readiness line for each AI engine; engines differ there only by what your robots.txt lets in. One fix arrives written out in full, in three parts: the immediate step, the task behind it, and a way to confirm it took. You get three ChatGPT runs a day per connection, and checking the same site again inside 24 hours returns the stored answer rather than a new one.

| Step | HubSpot grader | OperStack free check | OperStack Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engines answering | OpenAI model, Perplexity, Gemini | ChatGPT, search on | ChatGPT, search on |
| How often | Once | On demand, 3 ChatGPT runs a day | Every 7 days |
| Questions | Pre-set, count not stated | 3 | 10 |
| Your pages | No crawl described | Up to 10 read and scored | 10 scored every week |
| Rivals | Share of Voice points | Named, sorted by type | Named, plus 3 measured beside you |
| Output you act on | Interpretation, sample recommendations | One full fix, opening paragraph | Fix list with page address and text |

What OperStack leaves out, stated plainly: it does not score sentiment, it has no keyword database, search volumes, backlink index or rank tracking, and it does not collect Claude, Perplexity or Gemini answers each week.

## What does each tool tell a business owner on a Monday morning?

HubSpot's grader hands you a grade and a description of your reputation. OperStack hands you the names that were recommended instead of you and the sentences to paste.

From the grader, a Monday looks like this: a score out of 100 in five parts, a written interpretation, and a Market Competition label that places you as a Leader, Challenger or Niche Player and as an Innovator, Disruptor or Traditionalist.
