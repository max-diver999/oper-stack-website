## What are OperStack and Otterly.AI for?

OperStack Watch combines 10 weekly ChatGPT questions with page scoring and draft fixes for $35 monthly. Otterly Lite costs $29 monthly and tracks 15 prompts daily across 4 included engines. Our distinction is the written page work and scheduled inbox report; Otterly has the lower monthly entry price.

### OperStack: from ChatGPT's answer to a paste

<!-- cite-block:start -->
Type an address into the [free check](/ai-visibility/). With no account, it samples up to ten pages from your sitemap and scores them out of 100: can AI crawlers get in (robots.txt read for 14 crawlers and fetchers), is there an llms.txt map, is the business defined in schema, is there an answer-first paragraph worth quoting, are pages dated and sourced. It also gives an "SEO basics" score on the same pages. Then ChatGPT gets three questions a buyer in your field would ask, with web search on, and you see who it recommended.

OperStack Watch runs that loop every week for $35 a month. You get ten buyer questions, the names ChatGPT gave in place of yours, three rivals measured on the same scale, a fresh ten-page score, and one priority fix chosen from the sampled pages, pairing a page address with proposed text to review. The weekly email opens with what changed.
<!-- cite-block:end -->

### Otterly.AI: daily prompt monitoring from Austria

Otterly is made by OtterlyAI GmbH in Persenbeug, Austria. Its own description: an AI search monitoring and optimization platform where you define prompts, it runs them across AI search engines, and it reports mentions, sentiment, website citations, competitors and recommendations. It adds single-URL GEO audits, prompt research and Agent Analytics, which counts AI bot visits from server logs.

### Where does OperStack go further than Otterly?

**Otterly tells you how often AI engines mention you; OperStack goes on to tell you what to change on which page, and shows it to you before you sign up.** Seven differences, each checked against Otterly's own pages:

1. **Ten pages read, not one URL.** Otterly's help center is explicit that a GEO audit covers "one single URL page", even if you type in your domain, and bulk audits go through the API. The OperStack free check samples up to ten pages from your sitemap on every run, and Watch scores all ten again each week. A site's AI readiness rarely lives on the homepage alone; it lives on the service, pricing and location pages buyers land on.

2. **The paragraph, not a suggestion.** Otterly's recommendations come with a "Reasoning" and a "Suggestion", described as "a concrete action you can take", in types such as Create Content On-Page, Improve Existing Content, Reddit, press release and YouTube. Ready-to-paste page text is not stated. OperStack writes the text: for any checked page missing a short answer at the top, an opening paragraph assembled only from what your own pages say, with a button to copy it. Watch proposes one priority fix each week, then checks the site again.

3. **Enough data on day one.** Otterly refreshes recommendations every seven days, and only once an account has 15 or more prompts, three or more competitors defined and three days of data. Lite holds exactly 15 prompts and shows up to three recommendations per cycle. The OperStack free check writes its first fix in the same session you type your address.

4. **A live answer before any signup.** Otterly offers no free public check of live AI answers; seeing whether ChatGPT names you means signing up for the trial. Its free AI Brand Authority Check asks models whether they know a brand and says its results "reflect AI training data, not real-time web". Ours asks ChatGPT with web search on, while you wait, and you can run it three times per connection per day.

5. **Rivals and non-rivals kept apart.** Otterly's Prompts tab lists the competitor brands that appear in AI answers. OperStack names everyone ChatGPT recommended in your place and sorts them: actual competitors, businesses that only look like rivals (portals, law firms), and names it could not identify. A property portal that ChatGPT keeps mentioning stops cluttering your list of rivals.

6. **A letter instead of a login.** A weekly email is not stated anywhere in Otterly's pages we read; it mentions alerts when brand mentions change and notification toggles in settings. Watch sends one email every seven days from your subscription date, with the week's changes and the fixes.

7. **MCP for everyone.** Otterly's MCP server starts at Standard, $189 a month. OperStack's [MCP server](/mcp/) is free, needs no account, and scores or compares sites from inside Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other clients.

## How does Otterly check AI answers, and how does OperStack?

Otterly Lite follows 15 selected prompts daily across 4 engines and offers separate URL audits. Watch asks 10 ChatGPT questions weekly, samples website pages and writes proposed edits. Daily multi-engine tracking and weekly ChatGPT reports have different coverage; a side-by-side count alone cannot establish which produces more useful business decisions.

| Step | Otterly.AI | OperStack |
|---|---|---|
| Who writes the questions | You, with a prompt calculator to help; each prompt counts once per country | Free check: nothing to write, only your address; Watch: ten questions your buyers ask |
| How answers are collected | Scraped "the same way a real user would", every day | ChatGPT asked with web search on, weekly in Watch |
| Your site | One URL per GEO audit | Ten pages per check, scored out of 100 |
| Rivals | Competitors column, share of voice, average position | Every name given in your place, sorted into rivals and non-rivals; three rivals measured beside you |
| What you are handed | Charts, citations, sentiment, recommendations with a suggestion | A priority page fix: page address, then the paragraph |

Two practical consequences follow from this. Otterly's per-country counting means a clinic chain asking the same 15 prompts in two countries uses 30 prompt slots, twice the Lite allowance. And because the prompt list is yours, the quality of what Otterly measures depends on how well you guessed what buyers type. The free check skips that guess: it asks three questions a buyer of your kind of business would ask, with nothing for you to write.

On engines, Otterly covers more. Its base plans include ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot, with AI Mode, Gemini and Claude as add-ons; its changelog says Claude launched as early access for the first 50 customers. Watch puts its weekly questions to ChatGPT only. What the free check does for the other engines is narrower: it reads your robots.txt rules for 14 AI crawlers and fetchers and reports, engine by engine, whether their bots are let in. It does not track what those assistants answer.

## Otterly or OperStack for a clinic owner, an agency running ten clients, a software company or a shop?

For 4 common buyers, coverage and the person doing the work matter more than the plan name. Watch keeps ChatGPT questions and page edits together. Otterly includes daily observations across several engines. A clinic, agency, software company or shop should decide which questions it can realistically review and act on.

**If the goal is to be named in ChatGPT, each of these four starts with the OperStack free check; Otterly fits only where the job is reporting across many engines or markets.**

1. **A clinic owner**

A clinic owner rarely has time to write 15 prompts, define three competitors and wait three days for the first suggestion. With OperStack, the free check needs the clinic's address, then shows whether ChatGPT named it for three patient-style questions and which clinics it named instead. If a service page opens with a slogan instead of an answer, the paragraph to paste is already written. [Watch](/pricing/) keeps asking every week for $35 a month.

2. **An agency running ten clients**

An agency is paid either for reports or for results. For results, the OperStack kit is: a free check to start a pitch by showing a prospect which firms ChatGPT recommended over them; the free MCP tools, which put up to four sites on the same 100-point scale from inside your AI client; a one-off $149 audit when a client signs (100 pages read, five rivals, files ready to use, a person reviews it, no subscription attached); and a Watch subscription for each client whose weekly fixes your team will publish. Otterly Standard's 100 prompts across ten clients leave ten prompts each (15 under its agency partner program, which lifts Standard to 150), before any second country doubles the count.

3. **A software company**

SaaS buyers ask ChatGPT for shortlists, and the list is where a product is won or lost. Each week Watch asks ChatGPT ten questions your buyers ask, lists the tools named in your place, and writes the opening your product or pricing page lacks. Developer-led teams can run the free MCP tools inside the editor they already use. Two other comparisons SaaS teams read: [OperStack vs Profound](/compare/operstack-vs-profound/), a platform that pairs AI answer tracking with content agents, and [OperStack vs Semrush](/compare/operstack-vs-semrush/), a suite with keyword data.

4. **A shop**

Shoppers ask where to buy a product and which store is trustworthy. Watch shows whether the store appears and which shops ChatGPT preferred, and it writes the answer-first paragraph for the category page. Shops relying on AI ad reporting should note the paused ChatGPT Ads tracking at Otterly described above.

## Running both, or moving from Otterly to OperStack

Before switching, compare the questions and engines you use today with Watch's 10 weekly ChatGPT questions. Keep Otterly for any monitoring that still serves a decision. Save exports and check the billing period before cancelling; the products can also remain alongside each other when their outputs have distinct owners.

Otterly's MCP server, on Standard and above, returns your brand report data, prompts, raw answers, citations, recommendations and GEO audits, and can create or delete prompts with write permission. OperStack's free server scores a site out of 100, compares two to four sites on one scale, checks an llms.txt file and whether its links lead anywhere, and reads your AI visit counter. In one Claude or Cursor session you can pull the sites Otterly says AI answers cite, then put up to three of them next to yours on one scale with OperStack's compare tool.
