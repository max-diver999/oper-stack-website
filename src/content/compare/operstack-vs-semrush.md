## What is OperStack for, and what is Semrush for?

**OperStack answers one question for a business owner: when a buyer asks ChatGPT for a business like yours, are you named, who is named in your place, and what should your pages say instead. Semrush is a search marketing suite for SEO professionals, with AI visibility as one module inside it.** The two overlap in that module and almost nowhere else.

### OperStack: ChatGPT's answer about you, turned into this week's work

The [free check](/ai-visibility/) needs your address and nothing else: no account, nothing to install. It reads up to ten pages sampled from your sitemap and scores them out of 100 across five areas. Access is worth 25 points (robots.txt read per user agent for 14 AI crawlers and fetchers), the agent map 15 (llms.txt), the entity 20 (business schema, FAQPage, canonical, Open Graph), quotable content 25 (an answer-first paragraph with a figure under the H1, three or more H2 sections, a table) and dates and sources 15. Then it puts three questions a buyer of that kind of business would ask to ChatGPT, with web search on, and shows whether you were named and who was named instead.

OperStack Watch makes that a weekly routine for $35 a month. Every week: ten questions your buyers ask, the businesses ChatGPT recommended in your place, three rivals measured beside you, your ten-page score measured again, and a fix list for the whole site in which each item names the page and carries the text to paste. One email a week tells you what moved.

### Semrush: a search data suite that added AI answers

Semrush describes itself as powering "brand visibility across search and AI-driven discovery". Its core is keyword research, backlink analysis, rank tracking and site audits. Its company page says it passed 100,000 paying customers in 2023, serves 156 countries, and in 2026 became an Adobe company. AI visibility is sold two ways: as the AI Visibility Toolkit on its own, or inside the "SEO + AI Search" plans, which track prompts you choose across several AI systems and benchmark you against competitors.

## Where does OperStack do more than Semrush for AI visibility?

**On the narrow job of getting a business named in ChatGPT, OperStack goes further than Semrush's AI module in seven concrete ways, for about a third of the price of the cheapest Semrush option.** Each point is checked against Semrush's own pages as they stood on 26 September 2026.

## What does each tool tell a business owner on Monday morning?

**Semrush gives you a dashboard to interpret; OperStack gives you a short email with names in it and a paragraph to paste.**

Open Semrush's AI tools and you see an AI Visibility Score benchmarked against competitors, mentions and citations per platform, AI sentiment, prompts where competitors are cited and you are not, and Brand Performance's AI-generated strategic recommendations. If someone set up My Reports, a PDF also arrives on the chosen schedule, to up to 30 recipients on paid plans; a Pro Report at $20 a month adds white-label branding and AI summaries. It is a lot of signal. Someone still has to turn it into an edit.

A Watch letter is shorter because the translation is already done. It does not land on Monday by rule; it keeps the weekday you signed up on. The week printed above is the genuine first report for a Phuket property brand we run: five of ten answers named the site, two firms took three answers each in its place, and the fix arrived with a placement ("Add this to your homepage, just below the first section") and a paragraph assembled from the site's own facts. The owner's task that week was one paste on one page, then waiting for the next letter to see whether the answers moved.

In a business where nobody's job title contains "SEO", a dashboard score waits for an interpreter, while a named rival and a paragraph with a page address get handled the same afternoon.

## When you need a different kind of tool

Some jobs are not AI visibility at all, and OperStack does not do them. Keyword research is one: Semrush's stats page lists 28.9 billion keywords in 142 geo databases, and OperStack has no keyword database or search volumes. Link building is another: Semrush indexes 43 trillion backlinks, and we have no backlink index. Rank tracking is a third: Semrush plans follow 500 to 5,000 keywords a day in Google, and we track no rankings. Technical audits of large sites are a fourth: Semrush crawls 100,000 pages a month on Starter and runs more than 140 SEO checks, while our free check reads ten pages and the $149 audit a hundred.

If your team does that SEO work every week, a suite is the right tool for it. Whether ChatGPT names your business, who it names instead, and what to paste on which page is a separate job, and it is the one OperStack is built to do.
