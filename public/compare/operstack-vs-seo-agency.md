## What do OperStack and an SEO agency do?

OperStack Watch supplies 10 weekly ChatGPT questions, page checks and proposed edits for $35 monthly; the owner publishes the changes. An SEO agency sells human work under an agreed scope. Comparing them is mainly a decision about implementation capacity, with published provider surveys giving context rather than a quote for your site.

1. **A year costs less than one month of a typical retainer.** Watch is $35 a month, or $299 for twelve months. In the Ahrefs survey of 439 SEO providers, the most common monthly retainer band was $501 to $1,000. A full year of Watch therefore costs less than the bottom of that band for a single month.

2. **It measures the thing you are worried about.** Every week Watch puts the same ten buyer questions to ChatGPT with web search on, records a yes or a no for each, and lists every firm named in your place with a count. How an agency measures ChatGPT answers, if it does, is up to each agency: no named survey we found even states what share of small-business agencies offer AI-search work.

3. **The first look is free and needs no access.** Our [free check](/ai-visibility/) takes your web address and nothing else: no signup, nothing to install, no Search Console permissions, no call. It scores up to ten pages and shows what ChatGPT said in reply to three buyer questions. Backlinko, by contrast, lists site audits among standard agency projects at $1,000 to $5,000, and Google advises giving any auditor read access to Search Console only.

4. **The text comes from your own pages.** Each paragraph OperStack drafts is assembled from what your site already says, and every sentence is checked against a quote from your pages before it is allowed in. Google warns that deceptive or misleading content created for you by an SEO can get your site removed from its index. Our rule is built to avoid exactly that: nothing enters the draft that your own pages do not already support.

5. **Nothing is hidden.** Google lists secrecy, an SEO that will not clearly explain what it intends to do, among its warning signs. A Watch letter shows each question asked, each name ChatGPT gave, the score and the fix, so every line can be checked by you.

6. **No term to sit out.** Watch cancels at any time, and inside the first 7 days nothing is charged. Clutch says the usual SEO engagement in its verified reviews runs 12 months, and Ahrefs found 78.2% of providers bill by monthly retainer.

7. **No developer.** Writing fixes can be pasted into a page you already edit. The few that need code are marked, so you know exactly what to hand on and what to do yourself.

### What does an SEO agency do, and what does OperStack do?

An agency works on your site and your wider search presence; OperStack watches one assistant and tells you what to fix.

## How does each one find out whether ChatGPT names you?

Watch records 10 buyer questions and checks a sample of pages each week. An agency's AI method depends on its agreed scope, so request the questions, engines and reporting frequency in writing. A dated method makes its recommendations reviewable and lets you distinguish a monitoring service from implementation work.

| What you want to know | A typical SEO agency | OperStack Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Does ChatGPT name us? | Depends on its tools; no survey states a standard | Yes or no for each of 10 questions, weekly |
| Who does it name instead? | Depends on the agency | Every name, with a count across the 10 |
| How do we compare with rivals? | Usually part of strategy work | 3 rivals scored on the same scale |
| What exactly should change? | The agency decides, and often does it | Page address plus text to paste |
| Who makes the change? | The agency | You, or a developer for marked items |
| Google rankings and backlinks? | The core of the job | Not covered: no rank tracking or backlink index |

### Do I need an SEO agency for AI search?

Not to find out where you stand. Google's own hiring page says that if you run a small local business, "you can probably do much of the work yourself", starting with its SEO Starter Guide.

The same page now names "optimizing for generative AI" among the services an SEO may sell, and it tells owners to check that any AEO or GEO advice agrees with Google's official guidance on generative AI features. That gives you a simple test for any pitch: ask which Google document the advice rests on.

No named source in this review establishes a deadline for appearing in ChatGPT after an edit. An agency proposal should therefore specify work, observation dates and evidence, rather than a promised recommendation date.

## Should a clinic, a marketing agency, a SaaS company or a shop hire an SEO agency?

Across 4 business types, choose by implementation need. A clinic may edit its own service pages; an agency has client approvals; a SaaS team can assign work internally; a shop may face complex catalogue changes. Watch supplies a weekly observation and draft, while agency work requires a separate agreed deliverable.

1. **A dental or aesthetics practice.** With a single site, a practice is the small local business Google says can do much of the work itself. Start with Watch at $35 a month and paste the fixes yourself. Bring in an agency for a redesign, and budget with Ahrefs' local-market average of $1,557.08 a month in mind.

2. **A marketing agency with ten clients.** Here you are the agency, and the question is what you measure with. Watch gives each client a weekly ChatGPT reading and text your team can ship; ten subscriptions come to $350 a month, and our pricing page lists no bundle. Our free [MCP server](/mcp/) scores sites and compares two to four of them on one scale from inside Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, which helps in a pitch. Keep your suite for keywords and links; [OperStack vs Ahrefs](/compare/operstack-vs-ahrefs/) and [OperStack vs Semrush](/compare/operstack-vs-semrush/) explain that split.

3. **A SaaS company.** If growth depends on ranking for many keywords, that takes research, content and links, which is agency or in-house work. For AI answers, Watch tells you each week whether ChatGPT names your product when a buyer asks for tools in your category, and which products it names instead.

4. **An online shop.** Shops carry the technical risks on Google's list, redirects, error pages and JavaScript, most of all during a platform move, and that is a job for an agency. Day to day, Watch shows whether ChatGPT mentions the shop and supplies plain text for the pages it reads.

### How do you vet an SEO agency? Google's questions and red flags

Google's hiring page gives both a set of questions to ask and a list of warning signs. Use both before you sign, and keep an independent measurement running afterwards.

Ask for examples of previous work and success stories. Ask whether the agency follows Google Search Essentials, what results it expects and in what timeframe, how it measures success, and what experience it has in your industry, your country or city, and with international sites. Ask how long it has been in business, how you will communicate, and whether it will share every change with its reasoning. A good agency asks you questions too: what makes your business different, who your competitors are, how customers find you. Call its references. For an audit, grant read access to Search Console, never write access.

Google's warning signs:

1. An SEO firm that emails you out of the blue.
1. Any promise of first place on Google; Google says no one can promise that.
1. Claims of a "special relationship" with Google, or a "priority submit".
1. Secrecy about what the agency intends to do.
1. Deceptive or misleading content created on your behalf.
1. A request that you link to the agency: "You should never have to link to an SEO."
1. Link popularity schemes, or submitting your site to thousands of search engines.
1. Tools that claim to be approved by Google Search.
1. A request for write access to Search Console at the audit stage.

Google adds that you are responsible for what any company you hire does in your name. In the US, complaints go to the FTC; elsewhere, to econsumer.gov.

Bring a concrete page to an agency review rather than accusing a supplier from a score alone. Ask what question the opening answers, where each claim is supported and whether the current contract includes rewriting it. If the work is outside scope, request a separately priced task with approval and publication responsibilities.

A dental page illustrates why judgement is necessary. Adding a treatment deadline to satisfy a numerical rule would be inappropriate without evidence. A truthful opening might use an already published appointment price or a defined service area, but the practice must confirm that it remains accurate and suitable to publish.

Watch supplies observations and proposed wording that can help prepare that conversation. Keep the agency's implementation record beside the report, with the approved text and publication date.
